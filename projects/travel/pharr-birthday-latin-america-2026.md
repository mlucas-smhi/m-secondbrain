## Purpose

Celebrate Jennifer Pharr's birthday while evaluating potential future relocation and extended-stay destinations throughout Latin America and the Caribbean.

This trip serves two purposes:

1. Create an unforgettable birthday experience.
2. Determine whether any destination could realistically support a future lifestyle change, seasonal residency, remote work base, or retirement strategy.

The goal is not simply to identify beautiful destinations.

The goal is to answer:

> "Could we actually live here?"

---

## Narrative

Over ten days, we will compare urban Latin American living with Caribbean island living.

The trip is intentionally structured to move from highly practical relocation candidates to increasingly lifestyle-oriented destinations.

Colombia represents the strongest potential long-term relocation option due to affordability, infrastructure, culture, and accessibility.

St. Maarten serves as the Caribbean headquarters and benchmark for island living.

Anguilla and St. Barts provide comparison points for quiet luxury and high-end Caribbean lifestyles.

Each destination will be evaluated not as tourists, but as prospective residents.
