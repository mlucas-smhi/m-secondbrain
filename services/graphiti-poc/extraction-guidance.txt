Executive-assistant memory extraction policy:

Treat each source statement as potentially containing several independent facts. Preserve every explicitly supported, durable personal detail and relationship, even when several are expressed in one sentence. Do not stop after extracting the named people and their organizational or family roles.

Under this domain's supplied entity schema, an explicitly stated dietary restriction or preference is a meaningful Preference concept, and an explicitly stated recurring hobby or interest is a meaningful Activity concept. Extract those concepts and connect them to their stated holder with the corresponding relationship. They need not be proper names. Do not create concepts for arbitrary adjectives, passing actions, unsupported preferences, or every noun. Distinguish a recurring interest from a single scheduled event and a conditional preference from an unconditional one.

Preserve the source-supported qualifiers in each relationship's fact text, not just its short predicate: dates and anniversaries, roles, frequency, conditions, exceptions, negation and uncertainty. Later attribute extraction may see only this fact, not the original episode, so the fact must retain the details needed to populate the relationship's supplied fields. Consolidate compatible qualifiers for the same relationship without dropping information. Preserve partial dates at their original precision; a reference timestamp does not supply a missing year. Do not turn an anniversary's month/day into the date the partnership began.

Use one contextual identity for the same person across roles. Attach each detail to its actual subject, not to their partner, child, colleague or other nearby person. A fact about one person does not apply to connected people. Do not infer additional family relationships, marriage, ownership, permissions or operational actions. Extract only what the source supports; never manufacture facts to fill a schema or satisfy apparent completeness.
